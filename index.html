What It Does
Sales: answers product questions, recommends the best-fit product or bundle, and supports replenishment, upsell, and cross-sell. Service: handles order status, shipping, and returns, all in the same conversation.
What It Unlocks
Faster, consistent answers around the clock. It also follows up on abandoned carts and re-engages lapsed customers. Your team sees every thread and can take over at any time.
What We Need From You
A point of contact to approve milestones. Your product range with pricing, bundles, and subscriptions. Returns, shipping, and refund policies. A few sample conversations, plus your compliance guardrails and access to your systems when requested.
Guardrails & Go-Live
Guardrail: no health or medical claims; anything medical is escalated to a human.
Human hand-off: your team sees all threads and can take over anytime.
Tested first: every answer is reviewed and approved in a test environment before a real customer talks to the AI employee.

Commerce Funnel

The AI employee moves each customer through a defined commerce funnel, and every step is tracked in a single dashboard. It is a capability of the existing Sales & Service AI employee, not a separate one, and operates within Nutrillermo's existing Shopify checkout.

Reached → Engaged → Recommended → Checkout started → Purchased
Included
  • Stage tracking · each conversation/customer is tagged with its current funnel stage, populated automatically from the AI employee's activity and Shopify events.
  • Recovery & re-engagement · abandoned-checkout recovery and basic re-engagement (as already scoped), attributed back to the funnel.
  • Funnel dashboard · volume, conversion, and drop-off between each stage, plus revenue, filterable by channel and language (ES/EN).
  • Best-effort attribution · using the identifiers each channel provides (phone, email, Shopify customer).

Assumptions. Shopify checkout / abandoned-checkout webhooks and Klaviyo access are provided; WhatsApp message templates are approved by the Client; conversation volume stays within the agreed monthly threshold.
